# GPT-5 output recovery

Medium reasoning with8192 output tokens produced incomplete responses at batch64, and again at batch16. All dispatched attempts are retained. Unsent medium requests were withdrawn from the bridge allowlist to avoid further waste; rejections for those digests are administrative cancellations, not evidence of model inability.

The complete1842-case GPT-5 panel is rerun at low reasoning, batch64,8192 output tokens. Scientific prompts and cases are unchanged. No primary predictions are pooled across reasoning configurations, and no accuracy was used to select a recovery. Other models retain their frozen settings. This comparison therefore has an explicit model-specific recovery setting and does not isolate model generation at equal reasoning.

Some low-effort64-case batches also truncated. Those missing/incomplete batches were rerun as16-case low-effort batches. Only disjoint valid low-effort predictions enter the final panel; mixed batch sizes are disclosed. Medium-effort valid outputs remain excluded from primary scoring.
